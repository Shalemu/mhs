    Tourism & Hospitality

    A global tourism powerhouse with world-famous destinations and rising visitor numbers.

    Sector Overview

    Iconic Destinations & Investment Potential

    Tanzania is home to some of the world’s most iconic tourism assets such as the Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Kilimanjaro, and Zanzibar, making it a premier location for hospitality, hotels, eco-lodges, tour operations, and experiential tourism.

    Extended Overview

    Tourism Scale, Diversity & Growth Potential

    Tourism contributes significantly to Tanzania’s GDP and foreign exchange earnings. The country attracts millions of visitors annually, driven by:

    • Wildlife tourism
    • Beach tourism
    • Cultural and heritage tourism
    • Adventure tourism
    • Marine tourism
    • Conference and MICE tourism

    Zanzibar is rapidly growing as a luxury and beach tourism hub. Visitor numbers increase year by year as global awareness and flight connectivity improve.
