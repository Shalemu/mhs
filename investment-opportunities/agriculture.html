    Agriculture & Agribusiness

    Tanzania’s largest economic sector, powered by vast arable land, diverse climates, high-demand export crops, and strong government support for value addition.

    Sector Overview

    The Backbone of Tanzania’s Economy

    Agriculture employs over 60% of Tanzania’s population and contributes significantly to GDP and exports. The sector presents unmatched opportunities across primary production, agro-processing, storage, logistics, packaging, and export-oriented value chains.

    Extended Overview

    Agricultural Scale, Diversity & Growth Potential

    Tanzania has over 44 million hectares of arable land, abundant water resources, and multiple agro-ecological zones suitable for a wide range of food and cash crops.

    Agriculture remains a national priority under industrialization policies, with strong encouragement for investors to focus on value addition, export competitiveness, and food security.

    • High global demand for tropical and specialty crops
    • Government incentives for agro-processing investments
    • Expanding export markets (EU, Middle East, Asia)
    • Strong domestic population growth
    • Underutilized land with room for expansion
    • Large, trainable agricultural workforce
