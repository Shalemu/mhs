    Businesses & Organizations in Tanzania

    We support local entrepreneurs, SMEs, NGOs, professional firms, startups, and established companies across all regions of Tanzania.

    Running a business in Tanzania comes with unique realities: complex registration and compliance requirements, changing tax rules, need for proper documentation, importance of clean financial reporting, growing competition, digital visibility challenges, need for professional branding, gaps in organizational skills, and limited access to structured advisory.

    MHS bridges these gaps by offering all the core services a business needs under one roof: registration & compliance, tax and financial reporting, branding & visibility, digital presence, websites, staff training, research & impact studies, business writing and documentation.

    Common Challenges We Solve

    • Registering the right type of business
    • TRA compliance and tax clarity
    • Poor record-keeping
    • Weak branding and marketing
    • Low-quality business documentation
    • Inconsistent digital presence
    • Lack of access to professional design
    • Website gaps
    • Limited staff skills and training
    • Need for market or impact data

    Who We Serve

    Foreign & Diaspora Investors

    We help international and diaspora investors explore, enter, establish, and grow in the Tanzanian market with clarity, structure, and verified guidance.

    Investing in Tanzania from abroad comes with many questions: Where do I start? What are the real opportunities? Which structure should I use? How do I register a company with foreign ownership? What permits do I need? What are the incentives? Who can I trust locally? How do I avoid misinformation?

    MHS Consults exists to answer these questions, honestly, practically, and professionally. We help investors understand the landscape, verify opportunities, structure investments, comply with regulations, and connect with the right partners.

    Our investor services cover: Pre-entry advisory, Market intelligence, Company setup for foreign shareholders, Licensing & regulatory compliance, Work & residence permits, Feasibility studies, Project packaging, Stakeholder engagements, Land facilitation, Ongoing advisory and representation.

    We act as your local partner, ensuring you move confidently, safely, and strategically.

    Common Challenges We Solve

    • Limited access to verified information
    • Difficulty understanding regulatory frameworks
    • Navigating permits and approvals
    • Identifying credible local partners
    • Understanding the real investment environment
    • Packaging investment-ready projects
    • Risk of misinformation or unverified agents
    • Bottlenecks in government processes
    • Cultural and administrative barriers
