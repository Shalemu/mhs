    Government Incentives

    Tanzania offers competitive tax and regulatory incentives designed to attract and support long-term investment.

    Introduction

    Supportive Policies for Investors

    Investors benefit from supportive government policies such as tax exemptions, reduced duties, prioritized project facilitation, and protected investment rights. Tanzania’s incentive framework targets sectors that drive sustainable economic growth.

    Extended Overview

    Comprehensive Incentive Framework

    General Tax Incentives

    • Custom duty exemptions
    • VAT exemptions on certain capital goods
    • Corporate tax reliefs for specific sectors
    • Investment allowances and depreciation benefits
    • Import duty reliefs for machinery and equipment

    Strategic & Special Incentives

    • Enhanced tax exemptions for large-scale projects
    • Priority access to government services
    • Fast-track approvals
    • Dedicated facilitation teams
