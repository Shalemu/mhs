    Investment Reforms & Ease
    of Doing Business

    Tanzania continues to modernize its regulatory environment to attract investment, simplify processes, and strengthen investor confidence.

    Introduction

    Creating a More Investor-Friendly Environment

    Recent investment reforms have streamlined registration, licensing, taxation, and investment facilitation processes—reducing bureaucracy, improving transparency, and accelerating market entry.

    Extended Overview

    Modernizing the Investment Landscape

    Digitalization

    • Online company registration via BRELA
    • Online business and sector license applications
    • Digital tax systems through TRA
    • Mobile money integration for payments
    • Digital land administration systems (ongoing)

    Investment Facilitation

    • TISEZA reforms and restructuring
    • Streamlined incentive frameworks
    • Faster approval timelines
    • Improved investor protection mechanisms

    Business Environment

    • Reduced number of regulatory licenses
    • Harmonized fees and charges
    • Simplified compliance requirements
    • New industrial policies supporting manufacturing

    Together, these reforms significantly reduce operational friction, improve efficiency, and lower the cost of doing business for investors.
