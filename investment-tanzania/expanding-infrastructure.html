    Expanding Infrastructure

    Tanzania is investing heavily in transport, energy, water, ICT, and logistics infrastructure to support sustained economic growth.

    Introduction

    Building a Modern Investment Economy

    Major national infrastructure projects are transforming Tanzania into a modern, efficient, and investment-ready economy by reducing costs, improving connectivity, and strengthening productivity.

    Extended Overview

    Nationwide Infrastructure Expansion

    Transport Infrastructure

    • Standard Gauge Railway (SGR) linking Dar es Salaam to inland regions
    • Nationwide road expansions connecting all regions
    • Upgraded international and regional airports
    • New deep-sea ports at Bagamoyo and Mtwara

    Energy Infrastructure

    • Julius Nyerere Hydropower Project (2,115 MW)
    • Expanded natural gas production and distribution
    • Growing solar and wind power generation

    ICT Infrastructure

    • National broadband backbone fiber expansion
    • Improved digital connectivity across regions
    • Accelerated digital transformation across sectors

    Water & Irrigation

    • Construction of new dams
    • Irrigation schemes in key agricultural zones
    • Upgraded urban water supply systems

    These investments reduce operational bottlenecks, improve efficiency, and significantly lower costs for investors.

    Key Insights

    Infrastructure at Scale

    Massive Public Investment

    Government commits billions of dollars annually to infrastructure development.

    Faster Cargo Movement

    The SGR is expected to reduce cargo transit time by up to 50%.

    Port Capacity Growth

    Port expansions increase efficiency, handling capacity, and regional trade flow.

    Strategic Importance

    Infrastructure Unlocks Investment

    Expanding infrastructure strengthens Tanzania’s competitiveness and unlocks large-scale investment opportunities across sectors.

    • Logistics and transport services
    • Energy and utilities
    • Agriculture and agro-processing
    • Real estate development
    • Tourism
    • Export-driven industries
