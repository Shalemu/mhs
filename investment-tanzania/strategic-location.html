    Strategic Location &
    Market Access

    Tanzania offers unmatched access to regional and international markets through its location, ports, borders, and trade agreements.

    Introduction

    Gateway to Regional Markets

    Located on the East African coast, Tanzania serves as a natural gateway to over 450 million consumers across East, Central, and Southern Africa. Its ports, railways, and road networks connect businesses to regional and global markets efficiently.

    Extended Overview

    Geographic Advantage at Scale

    Tanzania’s geography provides investors with exceptional leverage in regional trade and logistics.

    The Port of Dar es Salaam — one of the largest and busiest in East Africa — connects international shipping routes to six landlocked countries:

    • Zambia
    • DR Congo
    • Malawi
    • Burundi
    • Rwanda
    • Uganda

    Tanzania is a preferred transit route for hydrocarbons, minerals, agricultural commodities, manufactured goods, and humanitarian cargo.

    Trade & Market Access

    • EAC — Access to 300M consumers
    • SADC — Access to 250M consumers
    • AfCFTA — Access to 1.3B consumers
    • Reduced tariffs and simplified movement
    • One of Africa’s largest combined free-trade markets
