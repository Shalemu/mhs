    Tax Consultancy

    Tax in Tanzania can feel complicated, technical, and stressful especially when regulations change or when compliance mistakes lead to penalties. MHS Consults simplifies tax for you. We explain things clearly, prepare your filings correctly, help you plan ahead, and ensure you remain fully compliant while avoiding unnecessary risks. Our tax team supports individuals, SMEs, corporates, NGOs, and foreign investors with both day-to-day and strategic tax matters.

    Tax Compliance, Explained Clearly

    Tax is not just about filing returns. It involves planning, understanding obligations, meeting deadlines, record-keeping, and positioning your business to reduce exposure to audits or penalties.

    Many businesses struggle with:

    • Wrong tax categories
    • VAT confusion
    • Poor record-keeping
    • Incorrect PAYE / P9 alignment
    • TRA queries and notices
    • Delayed filings
    • Mismatched invoices
    • Lack of tax planning

    Our approach blends strong technical tax knowledge with friendly, practical explanations that make everything easy to understand.

    We work with you to ensure your tax environment is clean, compliant, and optimized.
