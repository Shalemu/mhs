    Business Registration & Compliance

    Starting and operating a business in Tanzania requires more than simply filling forms. You need clarity, structure, and proper compliance. MHS Consults helps you move from uncertainty and guesswork to a fully registered, lawful, and professionally structured business that can grow, bid for tenders, attract partners, and operate confidently. We guide you through every regulatory step, ensuring accuracy, transparency, and full alignment with Tanzanian laws.

    Regulatory Complexity, Simplified

    Tanzania has multiple regulatory bodies — BRELA, TRA, OSHA, WCF, NSSF, TBS, TMDA, LGAs (Business Licenses), and sector-specific compliance systems. For many clients, navigating these requirements becomes confusing, inconsistent, or time-consuming.

    That’s where MHS comes in.

    • End-to-end Business and Company Registration
    • Proper Documentation
    • Approvals & Compliance workflows
    • Annual Requirements
    • Updates & Amendments
    • Ongoing Post-Registration Maintenance

    Businesses that operate without full compliance eventually face unnecessary penalties, blocked systems, TIN suspensions, licence problems, or missed opportunities.

    Our role is to shield you from these issues, giving your business a clean, compliant foundation that builds trust and long-term sustainability.

    OUR SERVICES

    What We Do

    We provide a full suite of business and corporate services to ensure your company is fully compliant, registered, and operating efficiently.

    • Registration Services: Company name search & reservation, Limited company registration (Local & Foreign-owned), Business name registration, NGO / Foundation Registration, Licence Applications
    • Tax & Statutory Registrations: TIN registration, VAT registration, PAYE, SDL & Withholding setup, EFD registration guidance
    • Compliance & Regulatory Requirements: OSHA Registration, WCF Registration, NSSF Compliance setup, TBS / TMDA where applicable
    • Corporate Maintenance: Annual returns filing, Company amendments, Minutes & resolutions drafting, Compliance audits (clean-up service)
