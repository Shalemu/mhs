Tanzania’s Finest Agricultural Products, Verified and Delivered Globally

We supply high-quality Tanzanian crops sourced directly from farmers, AMCOS, processors, and verified warehouses backed by strict quality assurance and seamless export coordination.

Tanzania’s Crop Power

Tanzania is one of Africa’s most naturally fertile agricultural hubs, producing globally demanded crops such as cashew, sesame, pigeon peas, spices, coffee, cocoa, sunflower, and legumes. CropLink makes these products accessible to global buyers through verified sourcing, quality inspection, accurate documentation, and professional export execution.

Below is an overview of the crops we supply, each representing the best of Tanzania’s agricultural potential.

Cashew Nuts (RCN & Kernels)

Tanzania is globally recognized for premium cashew nuts with high kernel yield, natural flavor, and strong nut count. Sourced mainly from Mtwara, Lindi, and Tanga, our cashews meet international grading and moisture standards. Preferred by buyers in India, Vietnam, UAE, Turkey, and Europe.

View Cashew Specifications

Sesame Seeds

Our natural sesame comes from Dodoma, Singida, Manyoni, Kondoa, and Morogoro — regions known for high purity, strong aroma, and excellent oil content. Tanzania’s sesame is especially valued in India, Turkey, UAE, and China for its color consistency and naturally low chemical use.

View Sesame Specifications

Pigeon Peas, Green Moong & Chickpeas

Tanzania’s chickpeas and green moong beans are appreciated for their bright color, high protein levels, and firmness. Clean, naturally dried, and uniform in size. Sourced from the Southern Highlands and Central Tanzania, ensuring consistent grain structure and color.

View Pigeon Peas Specifications

Sunflower Seeds

Grown in Singida, Manyara, and Dodoma, Tanzania’s sunflower seeds are known for high oil content and clean harvesting methods. They support both domestic and international edible oil production.

View Sunflower Specifications

Groundnuts (Peanuts)

Our redskin and runner-type groundnuts are sourced from regions with stable soils and favorable growing climates. They show low aflatoxin levels and reliable kernel uniformity, making them ideal for food processors and exporters.

View Groundnut Specifications

Avocado (Hass)

Tanzanian Hass avocados from Njombe, Kilimanjaro, and Iringa offer rich flavor, high creaminess, and excellent oil content. They are fast becoming a preferred export to Europe, Middle East, and Asia.

View Avocado Specifications

Coffee (Arabica & Robusta)

Tanzania’s coffee varieties, especially Arabica from Kilimanjaro, Mbeya, and Songwe, are renowned for balanced acidity, aromatic sweetness, and specialty-grade profiles. Robusta from Kagera offers bold character ideal for espresso blends.

View Coffee Specifications

Cocoa

Sourced primarily from Kyela, Tanzania’s cocoa is fragrant, clean, and naturally grown, highly valued by specialty chocolate makers for flavor depth and quality potential.

View Cocoa Specifications

Cloves

Tanzania’s cloves, particularly from Zanzibar, are among the strongest globally in aroma and oil content. They are preferred by spice processors, essential oil manufacturers, and exporters worldwide.

View Clove Specifications

Black Pepper

We supply aromatic black pepper from Morogoro, Tanga, and Zanzibar, appreciated for its warm, intense flavor and exceptional pungency.

View Black Pepper Specifications

Cardamom & Cinnamon

Tanzania produces naturally sweet, aromatic cinnamon and cardamom that is clean, aromatic, and bright in color with high essential oil content. Increasingly demanded by spice packers and tea manufacturers globally.

View Cardamom & Cinnamon Specifications

Beans (Red, Yellow, Black, Soya)

Tanzanian beans are known for their uniformity, good hydration, and clean harvesting. They serve both bulk export markets and food industries across Africa and Asia.
