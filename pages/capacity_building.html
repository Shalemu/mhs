Capacity Building & Export Training

Empowering people for export success and long-term sector growth.

Overview

CropLink invests in people as much as products. We train farmers, processors, and exporters in export readiness, quality standards, and documentation.

Through partnerships with development partners, government authorities, and crop boards, we build capacity that drives long-term sector growth and inclusion.

Key Offerings

  • Export compliance training
  • Traceability and certification workshops
  • Youth and women mentorship programs
  • SME export coaching
  • Diaspora exporter support

Why Choose CropLink

Because knowledge creates competitiveness and we share it freely.

Connecting Tanzania’s farmers and exporters to global markets through trusted sourcing, compliance, and trade facilitation.
