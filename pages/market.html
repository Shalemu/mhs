Connecting Tanzania’s Crops to Global Markets

We export high-quality agricultural products to buyers across Asia, Europe, the Middle East, and Africa supported by strong compliance, reliable logistics, and trusted sourcing networks.

Our Global Position

CropLink operates at the intersection of farm-level sourcing, export logistics, and international trade workflows, enabling seamless movement of Tanzanian products to global buyers.

Our market footprint covers countries with high demand for:

  • Pulses & oilseeds
  • Nuts & kernels
  • Spices
  • Fresh horticulture
  • Dry commodities
  • Premium specialty crops

Buyers value us for consistency, transparency, compliance, and professional communication essentials in global trade.

Why Buyers Choose CropLink

  • ✅ Reliable Supply – Strong sourcing networks across Tanzania’s major producing regions.
  • ✅ Accurate Grading – Every crop is inspected for moisture, purity, and uniformity.
  • ✅ Transparent Communication – Photos, videos, and sample confirmations before loading.
  • ✅ Compliance Confidence – Peri-shipment inspection, fumigation, COO, Phyto handled seamlessly.
  • ✅ Logistics Strength – Handled via Dar es Salaam port with dependable shipping partners.
  • ✅ Multi-Market Expertise – Understanding standards and preferences per region.
  • ✅ Integrity in Execution – No shortcuts, no mixed grades, no surprises.

How We Ship (Export Logistics)

Shipping Options:

  • FCL (full container loads)
  • Multi-crop shipments
  • Cold-chain reefer containers (for avocado)
  • Bulk-contract seasonal shipments

Container Capacities:

  • 20 ft: 16–20 MT (depending on crop)
  • 40 ft: 24–28 MT
  • Reefer 40 ft: 20–22 MT

Documents Handled:

  • Invoice & Packing List
  • Phytosanitary Certificate
  • Certificate of Origin
  • Fumigation Certificate
  • Insurance (on request)
  • BL instructions
  • SGS/Bureau Veritas inspections (where required)

Regions We Serve

Below is a detailed breakdown of all regions, what they buy, and why CropLink is trusted.

Asia

Asia is CropLink’s largest market cluster, driven by high consumption of pulses, spices, and oilseeds.

Key Countries We Serve:
  • India, Pakistan, Vietnam, China, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Malaysia, Indonesia
Products in Highest Demand:
  • Sesame, Pigeon peas, Green moong, Chickpeas, Red kidney beans, Groundnuts, Black pepper, Cardamom, Cinnamon, Coffee, Cocoa
Market Dynamics:
  • India & Pakistan buy large volumes of pulses & sesame.
  • Vietnam & China import sesame, groundnuts & soya.
  • Malaysia & Indonesia demand spices & cocoa.
  • Asian buyers prioritize consistency, accurate grading & moisture control — CropLink excels here.
Middle East & GCC

The Middle East imports Tanzanian crops at growing volumes due to strong food-processing industries and large diaspora communities.

Primary Destinations:
  • UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain
Products in Highest Demand:
  • Beans, Sesame, Pigeon peas, Black pepper, Cardamom, Cloves, Cinnamon, Cashew kernels, Avocado, Coffee, Cocoa
Market Dynamics:
  • High demand for clean, well-sorted beans & spices.
  • Strong purchasing power for premium grades.
  • Fast clearance & efficiency supported by CropLink logistics partners.
Europe

Europe values quality, traceability, and compliance above all making CropLink a natural fit.

Primary Destinations:
  • Netherlands, Germany, France, Italy, Belgium, Poland, UK
Products in Highest Demand:
  • Coffee (Arabica & Robusta), Cocoa, Avocado, Black pepper, Cloves, Sesame, Specialty pulses
Market Requirements:
  • Phyto compliance, food safety docs, traceability, moisture & defect consistency, cold chain reliability.
Africa

Africa’s intra-continent trade is rising, especially for regional food security markets.

Countries Served:
  • Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, DRC, Zambia, South Africa, Mozambique
Products in Highest Demand:
  • Yellow beans, Red kidney beans, Black beans, Soya beans, Sunflower seeds, Sesame, Spices, Pulses for relief programs
Why CropLink Fits:
  • Proximity, competitive pricing, fast delivery, high volumes, customs expertise, NGO support.
