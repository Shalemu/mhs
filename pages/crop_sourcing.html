Reliable, Transparent & Quality-Assured Agricultural Supply

Overview

Sourcing crops in Tanzania requires accuracy, field intelligence, and strong supplier networks. CropLink provides a full-spectrum sourcing service: we identify, assess, buy, prepare, and export crops on behalf of international buyers or exporters.

Our presence in key producing zones ensures transparent procurement, consistent quality, and structured execution from farm to port. We eliminate guesswork and deliver a reliable, traceable sourcing experience.

What We Do

  • 1 Field scouting and sourcing across major production zones
  • 2 Supplier identification and verification
  • 3 Sample collection, grading, and quality testing
  • 4 Negotiation and lot reservation
  • 5 Buying on behalf of clients
  • 6 Warehouse handling and stacking supervision
  • 7 Export preparation: bagging, labeling, weighing
  • 8 Container loading, sealing & fumigation coordination
  • 9 Shipment documentation & inspection management

How We Serve

  • 1 Receive buyer specs (grade, moisture, packaging, tonnage)
  • 2 Identify precise sourcing zones
  • 3 Verify suppliers and available lots
  • 4 Negotiate, purchase, and reserve cargo
  • 5 Conduct QC tests (FM, purity, moisture, grading)
  • 6 Arrange warehouse preparation & repackaging
  • 7 Supervise loading & container sealing
  • 8 Coordinate fumigation, inspection & documents
  • 9 Deliver export-ready shipment to shipping line

Documents, Standards & Compliance

  • Pre-export inspection requirements
  • Moisture & grading parameters
  • Packaging & labeling requirements
  • Fumigation & SPS checks
  • Export documentation set (Invoice, Packing List, Certificates)

Why Choose CropLink

  • Strong on-ground sourcing presence
  • Ability to purchase on behalf of clients transparently
  • Technical expertise in crop quality assessment
  • Reliable end-to-end export process
  • Protects buyers from poor-quality shipments and unreliable suppliers
