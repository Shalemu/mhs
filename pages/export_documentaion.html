Export Documentation & Compliance

Accurate, Complete, and Compliant Documentation for Smooth Export Flow

How We Serve

  1. Collect shipment details from supplier or buyer
  2. Prepare commercial documents accurately
  3. Arrange pre-export inspections and verification steps
  4. Coordinate certification (SPS, Fumigation, Origin, Weight, etc.)
  5. Submit documents to clearing agent & shipping line
  6. Cross-check BL draft with buyer requirements
  7. Compile final export file ready for buyer or bank submission

Documents, Standards & Compliance

  • Invoice & Packing List
  • Inspection Certificate
  • Phytosanitary Certificate
  • Fumigation Certificate
  • Certificate of Origin
  • VGM (Verified Gross Mass)
  • Bill of Lading Instructions & Draft
  • Export Clearance Files
  • Crop Quality & Packaging Standards
  • Destination-Country Requirements

Overview

Documentation is the backbone of every successful export. One missing detail or delayed certificate can stop your shipment at the warehouse, port, or destination country.

CropLink manages the entire documentation and compliance sequence with precision, ensuring every file is complete, accurate, and aligned with inspectors, shipping lines, clearing agents, regulators, and buyers.

We eliminate errors, miscommunication, and delays by preparing documents the way global buyers expect and regulators approve.
