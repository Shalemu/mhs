Export Documentation & Compliance

Your gateway to fast, accurate, and compliant export processing.

Overview

Proper documentation is at the heart of every successful export. CropLink prepares and manages all paperwork required for export approval and clearance.

We ensure accuracy, compliance, and timeliness that helps reduce the risk of penalties, rejections, or shipment delays.

Key Offerings

  • Invoice and packing list preparation
  • Certificates of origin and fumigation reports
  • Phytosanitary and export permits
  • Customs clearance and TRA e-filing
  • Document verification and record management

Why Choose CropLink

Because every delay costs money, and we eliminate them through precision and experience.

Connecting Tanzania’s farmers and exporters to global markets through trusted sourcing, compliance, and trade facilitation.
