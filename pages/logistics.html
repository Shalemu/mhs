Overview

Coordinating agricultural export logistics requires precision. Crops must be verified, packed, loaded, sealed, inspected, fumigated and delivered to port — all within tight timelines aligned to vessel schedules.

CropLink manages the full chain, ensuring quality, operational accuracy, and timely delivery. Our processes protect cargo integrity and ensure exporters and buyers remain aligned with shipping deadlines.

Key Offerings

Plan and schedule shipment timelines
Arrange inland transport to port
Supervise container loading, weighing, sealing
Coordinate fumigation processes
Oversee pre-shipment inspections
Manage shipping line documentation
Monitor vessel schedules & port updates
Confirm cargo integrity during handling

How We Serve

  1. Shipment planning aligned to timelines & requirements
  2. Warehouse preparation: stacking, labeling, weighing
  3. Loading supervision with photos, videos & seal confirmation
  4. Fumigation and inspection coordination
  5. Port delivery process: gate pass, terminal staging
  6. Shipping line documentation: SI, VGM, BL instructions
  7. Vessel loading confirmation & dispatch report

Why Choose CropLink

  • Reduces delays and minimizes wastage
  • Professional loading supervision preserves quality
  • Strong knowledge of port processes & timelines
  • Fully transparent communication for shipper & buyer
  • Reliable and predictable logistics execution
