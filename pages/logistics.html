Logistics & Shipping Coordination

Efficient, reliable, and fully managed shipping from warehouse to port.

Overview

CropLink oversees logistics from warehouse to port. We work with trusted transporters, freight forwarders, and shipping lines such as CMA-CGM, MSC, and Maersk, managing container booking, fumigation, and customs clearance.

Key Offerings

  • Container booking and scheduling
  • Warehouse management and loading supervision
  • Port coordination and inspection
  • Real-time shipment tracking
  • Post-shipment documentation and follow-up

Why Choose CropLink

Because we know Tanzania’s ports, routes, and systems, and we make exports move efficiently.

Connecting Tanzania’s farmers and exporters to global markets through trusted sourcing, compliance, and trade facilitation.
