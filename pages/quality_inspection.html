Quality Inspection & Certification

Ensuring every shipment meets the highest standards.

Overview

Quality defines Tanzania’s image abroad. CropLink coordinates quality inspection and certification through national and international authorities.

We ensure that every shipment is tested, graded, and traceable, meeting both buyer specifications and regulatory requirements.

Key Offerings

  • Sampling and laboratory testing
  • Moisture content and grading analysis
  • Certification through TBS, TMDA, TPHPA
  • Fumigation and inspection oversight
  • Pre-shipment verification and documentation

Why Choose CropLink

Because quality assurance isn’t a step, it’s our standard.

Connecting Tanzania’s farmers and exporters to global markets through trusted sourcing, compliance, and trade facilitation.
