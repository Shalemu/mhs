Quality Inspection & Certification

Standardized Sampling, Testing & Certification You Can Trust

Overview

Quality determines whether a shipment succeeds or fails in the global market. CropLink ensures every lot meets international standards through structured, professional quality inspection processes.

We conduct sampling, moisture testing, foreign matter checks, grading, purity verification, and final certification. Our inspections protect buyers from low-quality shipments and give exporters a strong foundation of trust.

What We Do

Pre-shipment sampling and grading
Moisture content testing
Foreign matter (FM) analysis
Kernel Outturn Ratio (KOR) analysis for cashews
Purity tests for sesame, legumes, spices
Color and grain uniformity checks
Laboratory testing (where required)
Inspection coordination with authorities
Issuance of detailed quality reports
Certification alignment with buyer specs

How We Serve

  1. Sample Collection, collected scientifically from various points
  2. Initial Assessment from grading, moisture, and FM checks
  3. Lab Testing for aflatoxin, oil content, purity, etc.
  4. Official Inspection arranged with relevant authorities
  5. Final Quality Report that is detailed, transparent, buyer-ready
  6. Approval For Shipment; only approved lots proceed to loading

Documents, Standards & Compliance Involved

  • Quality Analysis Report
  • Moisture and FM Sheets
  • Lab Certificates (when needed)
  • Pre-Shipment Inspection Certificate
  • Acceptance Confirmation

Why Choose CropLink

  • Ensures consistency, reliability, and buyer satisfaction
  • Protects cargo value and reduces rejection risk
  • Transparent, evidence-based inspection processes
  • Strong reputation with international buyers
