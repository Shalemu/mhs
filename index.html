About CropLink

CropLink Solutions Limited is a professional agribusiness and export facilitation company helping global buyers access high-quality Tanzanian crops with confidence.

We combine verified sourcing, quality inspection, structured documentation, and reliable shipping coordination to ensure every shipment is consistent, compliant, and delivered with precision.

Our mission is simple, to link Tanzania to the world by ensuring every farmer and exporter can compete confidently in global markets through professionalism, transparency, and strong partnerships.

Our Export Footprint

CropLink operates across Tanzania’s major agricultural zones covering the Southern, Central, and Coastal regions with hubs in Mtwara, Lindi, Tanga, Dodoma, and the Southern Highlands.

We export to Turkey, the UAE, Oman, India, China, and multiple European destinations, moving Tanzania’s finest cashew nuts, sesame seeds, legumes, cereals, spices, and horticultural products.

Commitment to Quality

Every shipment meets strict quality and traceability requirements in partnership with TBS, TMDA, and TPHPA, ensuring that Tanzanian products earn global recognition for consistency and integrity.
