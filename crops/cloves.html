Premium Tanzanian Cloves with High Oil Content, Strong Aroma, Export-Certified

CropLink supplies high-quality cloves sourced from Zanzibar and Pemba, globally renowned for strong aroma, high eugenol content, and superior flavor profile.

About Cloves

Tanzania, especially Zanzibar, Pemba, and Tanga coastal belts, is globally recognized as one of the world’s leading producers of premium-grade cloves.

  • High essential oil content (15–20%)
  • Strong aroma and warm fragrance
  • Intense flavor ideal for spice processors
  • Deep brown, oily appearance
  • Naturally sun-dried, hand-picked quality
  • Trusted reputation across Asian and Middle Eastern markets

Why Tanzania’s Cloves Are Superior

  • Exceptionally High Oil Content (15–20% Eugenol)
  • Complex, Warm, Long-Lasting Aroma
  • Clean Sun Drying
  • Strong Natural Color (deep brown/reddish)
  • Minimal Chemical Use (mostly organic)
  • Consistent Quality

Major Growing Regions

  • Zanzibar (Unguja) — premium grade; highest volumes
  • Pemba Island — aromatic cloves, historical origin
  • Tanga Coastline — moderate levels but excellent quality
  • Kilimanjaro (small pockets) — limited production but clean quality

Harvest Season & Availability

Main Harvest Season (Zanzibar & Pemba): September – January

Peak Buying Window: October – December
