Aromatic Tanzanian Black Pepper that is Bold Flavor, High Pungency, Export-Certified

CropLink supplies premium sun-dried black pepper sourced from Tanzania’s spice-growing regions known for strong aroma, high volatile oil content, and clean post-harvest handling.

About Black Pepper

Tanzania produces distinctive black pepper that competes strongly with Indian, Vietnamese, and Indonesian origins.

  • Sharp pungency
  • Warm, spicy aroma
  • Naturally sun-dried production

Why Tanzania’s Black Pepper is Superior

  • Strong Aroma & Pungency
  • High Volatile Oil Content (2–4%)
  • Naturally Sun-Dried

Major Growing Regions

  • Morogoro — largest and most consistent production
  • Tanga — coastal climate creates strong aroma
  • Zanzibar — small volume but premium quality
  • Pwani Region — emerging producer

Harvest & Availability

Main Harvest Season: July – October | Peak Buying Season: August – October

Quality Specifications & Grading

  • Moisture: ≤ 12%
  • Volatile Oil Content: 2–4%
  • Density: 500–550 g/L (Grade A)
  • Foreign Matter: ≤ 1%
  • Light Berries: ≤ 2%
  • Mold/Insect Damage: <1%
