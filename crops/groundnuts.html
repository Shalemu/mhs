High-Quality, Low-Aflatoxin Tanzanian Groundnuts for Global Food Markets

We supply clean, naturally dried groundnuts sourced from verified farmers and AMCOS across Tanzania tested for moisture, defects, aflatoxin, and export-grade purity standards.

About Groundnuts

Tanzania produces some of East Africa’s most natural and flavorful groundnuts. With strong production in Singida, Manyara, Dodoma, Shinyanga, and Tabora, our peanuts are:

  • Naturally sun-dried
  • Rich in oils
  • Well-formed with good kernel size
  • Low in chemical exposure
  • Suitable for food & industrial uses

Groundnuts are used in confectionery, roasting, peanut butter manufacturing, oil pressing, and food ingredient industries globally. CropLink ensures strict compliance with aflatoxin limits, moisture control, and cleanliness.

Why Tanzania’s Groundnuts Are Competitive

  • Naturally Grown, Low Chemicals
  • Strong Flavor & Oil Profile
  • Good Uniformity (Runner & Redskin types)
  • Strong Drying Conditions
  • Competitive Prices
  • Clean Handling (When Managed Properly)

Quality Specifications & Grading

  • Moisture: ≤ 10% (Ideal 6–8%)
  • Purity: 98%+
  • Admixture: ≤ 2%
  • Foreign Matter: ≤ 1%
  • Broken Kernels: ≤ 2–3%
  • Damaged Kernels: ≤ 3%
  • Aflatoxin: Within acceptable international limits

Varieties: Redskin, Runner-Type, Virginia-Type (small volumes)

Grades: Machine Cleaned, Handpicked Selected (HPS), Oil Extraction Grade
