Pigeon Peas (Toor Dal Raw Material)

Overview

Tanzania’s pigeon peas cream, white, and light brown varieties, are highly demanded for dhal processing in India and Pakistan. The grains are uniform, naturally dried, and retain strong structure during milling.

Specifications

  • Purity: 97–99%
  • Moisture: ≤ 12%
  • Admixture: 1–2%
  • Color: Cream / White / Light Brown
  • Size: Uniform grain, suitable for dhal splitting
  • Broken: < 3%

Buyer Markets

  • India (main market)
  • Pakistan
  • UAE
  • Nepal
  • Bangladesh (seasonally)

CropLink Buyer Support

  • Pre-shipment sampling
  • Grain size & color verification
  • Moisture testing
  • Cleaning & sorting supervision
  • Loading inspection with photos/videos
  • Full export documentation
