Specialty-Grade Tanzanian Coffee, Premium Arabica & Bold Robusta

CropLink supplies high-quality Arabica from Tanzania’s highlands and strong-bodied Robusta from the Kagera basin professionally processed, graded, and export-certified.

About Coffee

Tanzania is one of Africa’s most respected coffee origins known for floral, aromatic Arabica from high-altitude regions and rich, chocolatey Robusta from Lake Zone districts.

  • High-altitude Arabica (1,200–2,000m)
  • Naturally rich volcanic soils
  • Hand-picked harvest
  • Washed & natural processing
  • Balanced acidity and sweetness
  • Strong aroma profiles
  • Consistent bean size and density

Why Tanzania’s Coffee is Superior

  • High-Altitude Growing Conditions (Kilimanjaro, Mbeya, Songwe)
  • Unique Flavor Notes: citrus, berry, chocolate, floral, winey
  • Hand-Picked Harvest for ripe cherries
  • Multiple Processing Methods: Washed, Natural, Honey
  • Traceability through AMCOS and estates
  • Strong global reputation in specialty blends

Coffee Growing Regions

Arabica Regions:

  • Kilimanjaro — floral, bright acidity
  • Mbeya — balanced, chocolatey
  • Songwe — sweet, winey
  • Arusha — aromatic, medium acidity
  • Iringa & Njombe — emerging specialty zones

Robusta Regions:

  • Kagera — bold, chocolate-like, full-bodied
  • Bukoba — traditional East African Robusta profile
