High-Quality Tanzanian Pulses for Global Food & Processing Markets

CropLink supplies premium pigeon peas, green moong, and chickpeas sourced from Tanzania’s top agricultural regions, fully verified for purity, moisture, grain size, and international grading standards.

About Tanzania’s Pulses

Tanzania is a leading East African supplier of naturally grown pulses with strong demand across India, Pakistan, UAE, and global dhal processors.

Our pulses stand out for natural sun-drying, high protein content, uniform grain structure, stable color, low pesticide use, and strong cooking characteristics.

CropLink ensures these pulses are sourced responsibly from cooperatives, farmers, and verified warehouses, with strict quality checks before export.

Pigeon Peas (Toor Dal Raw Material)

Tanzania’s pigeon peas (cream, white, and light brown varieties) are highly demanded for dhal processing in India and Pakistan. The grains are uniform, naturally dried, and hold strong shape during milling.

  • Purity: 97–99%
  • Moisture: ≤ 12%
  • Admixture: 1–2%
  • Color: Cream / White / Light Brown
  • Size: Uniform grain, suitable for dhal splitting
  • Broken: < 3%

Buyer Markets: India, Pakistan, UAE, Nepal, Bangladesh

CropLink Support: Sampling, verification, moisture testing, cleaning/sorting supervision, loading inspection, export documentation
