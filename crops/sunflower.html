High-Oil Tanzanian Sunflower Seeds

CropLink supplies clean, naturally dried, and high-oil sunflower seeds sourced from Tanzania’s leading production zones with strict moisture control, impurity checks, and export-grade preparation.

About the Crop

Sunflower is one of Tanzania’s strongest agricultural value chains, supplying domestic edible oil manufacturers and international processors with high-oil, naturally grown seeds.

CropLink sources consistent-quality seeds from farmers, AMCOS, and processors in central regions with:

  • High oil content (38–45%)
  • Low moisture from natural sun-drying
  • Good grain uniformity
  • Low aflatoxin risk
  • Strong drying climate & low post-harvest humidity

Why Tanzania’s Sunflower Seeds Are Superior

  • High oil content (38–45%)
  • Naturally sun-dried, stable moisture
  • Low aflatoxin vulnerability
  • Stable production volumes
  • Ideal for oil processors
  • Clean handling, minimal chemical use

  • Shinyanga

Quality Specifications & Grading

  • Oil Content: 38–45%
  • Moisture: ≤ 10% (ideal 7–9%)
  • Impurities: ≤ 2–3%
  • Broken Seeds: ≤ 3%
  • Admixture: ≤ 1%
  • Foreign Matter: ≤ 2%
  • Aflatoxin: Within limits

How CropLink Supports Buyers

  • Verified sourcing from Singida, Dodoma & Manyara
  • Oil content & moisture testing
  • Grain uniformity assessment
  • Cleaning, sorting, bagging & weighing supervision
  • Warehouse prep & loading inspection
  • Full documentation (COO, Phyto, Fumigation, PSI)

Packaging & Shipment Options

  • 50 kg PP bags (standard)
  • 25 kg bags (on request)
  • Jumbo bags for bulk processors

Container Loading: 20 ft – 18–19 MT, 40 ft – 26–28 MT

Shipment Models: Single-container, multi-container seasonal, yearly contracts
