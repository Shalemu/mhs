Premium Tanzanian Cinnamon that is Sweet Aroma, Clean Bark, High Oil Content

CropLink supplies naturally grown cinnamon sourced mainly from Tanzania’s spice islands and coastal regions known for its warm aroma, sweet undertones, and clean bark structure.

About Cinnamon

Tanzania produces high-quality Cinnamomum verum (true cinnamon) and cassia-type cinnamon in various pockets. It is used globally for:

  • Spice blends
  • Bakery & confectionery
  • Beverage infusion
  • Essential oil extraction
  • Cosmetic & aromatherapy industries

Tanzanian cinnamon is valued for its sweet aroma, smooth quills, and clean bark free from dust and mold.

Why Tanzania’s Cinnamon is Superior

  • Sweet, warm flavor
  • Strong, consistent aroma
  • Naturally grown
  • Clean bark structure
  • Naturally sun-dried

Growing Regions

  • Zanzibar — historically strong spice producer
  • Pemba Island
  • Tanga Coastal Belt
  • Morogoro (forest pockets)

Quality Specifications

  • Moisture: ≤ 12%
  • Quill Length: 8–22 cm (or custom)
  • Thickness: Uniform
  • Foreign Matter: <1%
  • Color: Golden brown to dark brown
  • Aroma: Warm, sweet, strong
  • Oil Content: 1–2%

Grades Offered: Cinnamon Quills, Cut Pieces, Chips, Powder (on request)
