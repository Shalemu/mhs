High-Quality Tanzanian Beans. Clean, Naturally Dried, Export-Grade

CropLink supplies premium dry beans sourced from Tanzania’s leading farming belts — professionally cleaned, graded, and handled to meet strict global export standards.

About Tanzania’s Beans

Tanzania is one of East & Southern Africa’s most reliable origins for high-quality, naturally dried edible beans. Known for:

  • Uniform grain size
  • Clean sorting
  • Low moisture
  • Good cooking qualities
  • Natural sun drying
  • Consistent supply volumes

CropLink works directly with farmers, AMCOS, and commercial growers to supply export-ready beans compliant with food safety and market requirements.

Why Tanzania’s Beans Are Superior

  • Naturally Sun-Dried
  • Low Chemical Exposure
  • Clean, Well-Sorted Grains
  • Strong Seasonal Volumes
  • Competitive Prices
  • Excellent Cooking Quality

Growing Regions

  • Mbeya — high-quality kidney & yellow beans
  • Songwe — consistent volumes, low moisture
  • Ruvuma — premium red kidney and yellow
  • Manyara — black beans & mixed varieties
  • Kigoma / Kagera — strong on soybeans
  • Dodoma / Singida — drought-resilient varieties

Request Beans Prices & Specs

Red Kidney Beans (RKB)

  • Overview: Deep red color, strong grain structure, excellent cooking quality.
  • Specifications: Moisture ≤ 13%, Purity 98–99%, FM ≤ 1–2%, Size 180–220 grains/100g
  • Uses: Consumer retail, canned food processors, bulk distribution

Yellow Beans

  • Overview: Golden-yellow color, soft cooking texture, strong demand in East Africa & Middle East.
  • Specifications: Purity ≥ 98%, Moisture ≤ 13%, Uniformity ≥ 95%, Damage ≤ 3%
  • Key Markets: India, UAE, DRC, Rwanda, Burundi, Mauritius, Seychelles

Black Beans

  • Overview: Highly nutritious, rich in protein, clean & bold-colored.
  • Specifications: Moisture ≤ 13%, Color Deep Black, FM <1%, Broken <3%
  • Key Markets: South Africa, Asia, Middle East, Food manufacturers

Soya Beans

  • Overview: Good protein content, natural drying, low foreign matter.
  • Specifications: Moisture ≤ 13%, Protein 36–40%, Impurities ≤ 2%, Oil Content 17–20%
  • Key Markets: India, China, Zambia, Kenya, UAE
  • Uses: Oil pressing, animal feed, tofu & soy milk, food industry
