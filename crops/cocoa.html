Premium Tanzanian Cocoa, Rich Flavor, Clean Fermentation, Export-Certified

CropLink supplies high-quality fermented and dried cocoa beans sourced mainly from Kyela known for clean flavor, strong aroma, and consistent fermentation practices suitable for chocolate makers and processors.

About Cocoa

Tanzania is home to one of East Africa’s most promising cocoa origins especially from Kyela district (Mbeya region), where fertile volcanic soils and tropical rain patterns create cocoa with:

  • Rich chocolate notes
  • Strong cocoa aroma
  • Low bitterness
  • Clean fermentation
  • High butter content
  • Consistent bean size

Why Tanzania’s Cocoa is Superior

  • Unique Flavor Profile: bright acidity, fruity undertones, cocoa depth
  • Clean Fermentation Practices (3–7 days)
  • Strong Aroma Retention
  • Natural, Low-Chemical Farming
  • Consistent Bean Size
  • Volcanic Soil & Tropical Climate

Growing Regions

  • Kyela (Mbeya Region) — main production hub; premium flavor
  • Rungwe — secondary high-quality zone
  • Mbozi — emerging production pockets
  • Kigoma — small quantum, improving quality
