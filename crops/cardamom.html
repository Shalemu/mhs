Aromatic Tanzanian Cardamom that is Fresh, Green, and Export-Certified

CropLink supplies premium green cardamom grown in Tanzania’s high-altitude zones — known for bold aroma, natural green color, and high volatile oil content demanded by specialty spice buyers.

About Cardamom

Tanzania produces small but premium-quality cardamom in its highlands and tropical forest areas. Known for vibrant green pods, strong fragrance, and warm, sweet flavor, Tanzanian cardamom is suitable for:

  • Spice blends
  • Tea and chai masala
  • Middle Eastern sweets
  • Bakery flavoring
  • Essential oil extraction
  • Premium retail packaging

Why Tanzania’s Cardamom is Superior

  • Strong, long-lasting aroma
  • Natural green color
  • Ideal growing conditions
  • Minimal chemical use
  • Uniform pod size

Growing Regions

  • Tanga (Usambara Mountains) — main production zone
  • Morogoro — deep green pods, warm aroma
  • Kilimanjaro — small volume but premium quality
  • Zanzibar — pockets of forest-grown crop

Quality Specifications

  • Color: Deep green
  • Pod Size: 6–8 mm
  • Moisture: ≤ 12%
  • Foreign Matter: <1%
  • Aroma: Sweet, warm, strong
  • Volatile Oil Content: 3–5%
  • Uniformity: 95%

Grades Available: Super Green (Premium), Bold Green (Grade A), Mixed Green (Grade B)
