Premium Tanzanian Green Moong (Mung Beans)

Tanzanian green moong is naturally grown with bright green color, high protein content, and uniform grain size, ideal for dhal, sprouting, flour, and food processing industries.

Overview

Tanzanian green moong is valued for its bright green color, high protein content, and uniform grain size, perfect for dhal, sprouting, and food processing industries.

Specifications

  • Purity: 98–99%
  • Moisture: ≤ 12%
  • Color: Bright Green
  • Admixture: < 2%
  • Broken: < 3%
  • Use: Dhals, sprouting, flour production, snacks

Buyer Markets

  • India
  • Sri Lanka
  • UAE
  • Indonesia
  • Vietnam
