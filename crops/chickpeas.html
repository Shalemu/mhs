Premium Tanzanian Desi Chickpeas

High-quality desi chickpeas from Tanzania, ideal for dhal (chana dhal), flour production (besan), and food ingredient industries, known for stable color, strong grain structure, and consistent hydration.

Overview

Tanzania produces high-quality desi chickpeas used for dhal, flour production, and food ingredients. Known for stable color, good hydration, and strong grain structure.

Specifications

  • Purity: 98–99%
  • Moisture: ≤ 12%
  • Color: Light brown / Beige (Desi)
  • Broken: < 2–3%
  • Admixture: ≤ 1–2%

Buyer Markets

  • India
  • Pakistan
  • UAE
  • Saudi Arabia
  • South Africa

CropLink Buyer Support
